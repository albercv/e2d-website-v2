## What we do

We build bespoke software for companies. Code written specifically around each business's real process — not templates, not generic SaaS that the company has to bend to fit. We solve concrete operational frictions with custom development, automation, and AI where it delivers measurable ROI.

## Services, by priority

1. **Bespoke software development** (our primary service). Internal applications, dashboards, management consoles, integrations, operational tooling. Designed and engineered around the client's process. The company owns the asset — no per-user licences, no vendor lock-in.
2. **Process automation**. We eliminate repetitive manual work: quotes, commercial follow-ups, data entry, reporting, cross-system flows. We connect CRM, Sheets, Calendar, ERPs, and databases.
3. **Custom AI solutions**. AI embedded where it produces a real outcome: document classification, data extraction, intelligent search, content-generation pipelines, analytics. On-premise or local whenever the data demands it.
4. **Conversational agents** (a complement, not the business). WhatsApp chatbots and voicebots wired to calendars and CRMs when there is a real case for 24/7 acquisition or qualification. A piece inside broader solutions.

## Who it is for

Spanish SMEs of 10–50 employees with budget to invest in software. Priority sectors: aesthetic clinics, real estate agencies, accounting and consulting firms, professional-services SMEs. Typical buyer: general manager or owner, ROI-driven, concerned about GDPR, technical complexity, cost, and timelines.

## Why bespoke and not templates

Generic software forces the business to bend to fit the vendor's mould. That kills adoption, multiplies manual exceptions, and leaves the company tied to a licence that does not cover what they actually need. Bespoke software does the opposite: it adapts to how the business works today, targets the exact point where time or money is being lost, and remains an asset of the company.

Bespoke also means control: the code lives in the client's infrastructure when data sensitivity requires it, the flows respect the business's language and exceptions, and the roadmap is not hostage to a third-party vendor. The upfront investment is offset by a lower total cost of ownership over the medium term, and by an asset the business can keep exploiting, extending, or selling.

## Key messages

Time saved, fewer errors, 24/7 acquisition and response, lower operational load, better commercial follow-up, GDPR compliance by design, software that works in production because it was built for the real case.
